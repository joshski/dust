# Make Software Development Joyful

Dust exists because building software should be a source of joy, not frustration.

When development is joyful, people do their best work. They take on ambitious projects, maintain high standards, and sustain their effort over time. Joy comes from flow states, creative expression, meaningful collaboration, and seeing ideas become reality without unnecessary friction.

The opposite - isolation, tedium, fighting unmaintainable code, context-switching overhead - drains energy and leads to burnout, shortcuts, and abandoned projects.

Everything dust does serves this purpose.
